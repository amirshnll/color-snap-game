# Privacy Policy for Color Snap

Color Snap does not collect personal information, browsing history, website content, location data, or advertising identifiers. The game runs entirely within the extension popup and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Game data is not sent to a server, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Data Stored

The extension stores only game settings and records, including language, sound preference, accessibility mode, best score, and accuracy record. This information is stored locally in the browser and is removed when the extension data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

## Why the `storage` Permission Is Required

The `storage` permission is used only to preserve the settings and records listed above between sessions. It is not used to access personal files, browsing history, or website data, and it does not transmit information over the internet.

## External Link

The donation link opens an external website only when selected by the user. The privacy policy of that website applies after the user leaves the extension.
